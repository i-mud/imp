# Code of conduct

Imp's project spaces should remain useful, technical, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- discuss ideas and code on their merits;
- give actionable, specific feedback;
- assume good faith where reasonable;
- respect differing experience levels and backgrounds; and
- keep disagreements focused on the work rather than the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, deliberate
disruption, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information without
permission are not acceptable.

## Scope

This applies to project-controlled spaces including issues, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, and other official Imp community channels.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or block
accounts when necessary to protect the project and its participants.

For conduct concerns that should not be raised publicly, contact
`tuataur@googlemail.com`.

Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible.
